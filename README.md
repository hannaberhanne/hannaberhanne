# Hi, I'm Hanna

I build tools that turn messy operational data into decisions people can act on. Computer Science at the University of Tampa, Service Desk Analyst at Grant Thornton.

## Projects

**[Glide+](https://github.com/hannaberhanne/glide-plus)**
AI task manager for students who feel overwhelmed. Product lead on a 4-person team. Surveyed 35+ students, turned the results into the feature set, and shipped it to real users.
React, Node.js/Express, Firestore, GPT-4o-mini, Canvas LMS

**[Catalyst](https://github.com/hannaberhanne/Catalyst)**
Flags IT tickets headed for an SLA breach early enough to act. Catches 60%+ of the rare tickets that went on to breach, and shows what drives them.
Python, scikit-learn, SHAP

**[Shift-Adjusted Performance Model](https://github.com/hannaberhanne/shift-adjusted-performance)**
Scores service desk technicians on the person, not the schedule, by accounting for night, weekend and solo shifts. Adopted by UTampa ITS management as the standing evaluation method.
Python

**[Handwriting Recognition](https://github.com/hannaberhanne/handwriting-recognition)**
CNN and MLP models that read handwritten town names in historical records from Ghana. 93% on the EMNIST Letters benchmark, about 80% on real archival samples. Presented at NCUR 2026.
PyTorch, FastAPI, React

**[ITSM Playbook](https://github.com/hannaberhanne/itsm-playbook)**
Turns a service desk's ticket history into deterministic issue models, and the base for an AI call-guidance assistant I use on live support calls.
Python
